# 📊 Impact of Discounts and Offers on Consumer Buying Behaviour

A comprehensive market research project that analyzes how discounts and promotional offers influence consumer purchasing decisions, brand loyalty, impulse buying, and business profitability. This study combines primary and secondary research to provide actionable recommendations for sustainable discount strategies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Businesses widely use discounts and promotional offers to attract customers and increase sales. However, frequent discounting may reduce profit margins, weaken brand value, and create price-sensitive customers.

This research investigates the impact of different discount strategies on both consumers and businesses through survey analysis, case studies, and market observations.

---

## 🎯 Research Objectives

- Analyze the influence of discounts on consumer buying behavior.
- Study the relationship between discounts and impulse buying.
- Evaluate the effect of discounting on brand loyalty.
- Examine the impact of discounts on business profitability.
- Identify the most effective promotional strategies.
- Recommend sustainable and data-driven discount models.

---

## ❓ Research Problem

Businesses often rely heavily on discounts to increase sales without understanding their long-term effects.

This study addresses questions such as:

- Do discounts increase customer loyalty?
- Which discount strategy works best?
- Can excessive discounting damage brand value?
- How can businesses balance sales growth and profitability?

## 📊 Key Findings

### Consumer Insights

- 75% of customers purchase discounted products frequently or occasionally.
- 45% prefer percentage discounts.
- 30% prefer Buy One Get One (BOGO) offers.
- 50% remain loyal to brands offering regular discounts.
- 30% switch brands when competitors provide better offers.

### Business Insights

- 60% of businesses reported reduced profit margins due to discounts.
- 55.2% use discounts mainly to attract new customers.
- Major challenges include:
  - Reduced profit margins
  - Customer dependency on discounts
  - Brand devaluation

---

## 📈 Major Insights

- Discounts significantly increase short-term sales.
- Excessive discounting creates price-sensitive customers.
- Frequent promotions reduce perceived brand value.
- Percentage discounts and BOGO offers generate the highest customer response.
- Sustainable discount strategies outperform continuous price reductions.
